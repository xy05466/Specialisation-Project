# Specialisation-Project
 
WASD for movement 
E to interact with doors/keys/people 
U and O in overworld to rotate camera
J and L to change targets in combat 
U and O to change party to target in combat 
K to confirm target to attack 
Escape to instantly end battle

Collect all the keys in the stage to unlock locked door and fight boss

I to open menu
Click on item to move them
Hold shift while clicking item to split stack
Right click on player status while item is held to use item on player
Click outside of inventory to dispose

Continue button does not work on title screen, to reload progress click on continue and open menu before clicking on load button
